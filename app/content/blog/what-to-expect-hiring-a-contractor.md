If you've never had a lot professionally striped before, here's a walk-through of what the process actually looks like from quote to completion.

<!--more-->

## 1. Site visit & quote

We start with a walk-through of your property to measure the lot, note any ADA or fire-lane requirements, and understand your traffic patterns. You'll get a written quote with no surprise add-ons.

## 2. Scheduling

We work around your business hours whenever possible — early mornings, evenings, or weekends — to minimize disruption.

## 3. Surface prep

Before painting, we sweep and clean the surface so paint adheres properly and lasts as long as it should.

## 4. Layout & striping

We lay out stalls, fire lanes, arrows, and ADA-compliant accessible spaces according to code, then apply fast-drying traffic paint.

## 5. Cure time & walkthrough

Most paint is dry to the touch within an hour, with full cure happening over the following day. We do a final walkthrough with you to make sure everything meets expectations.
