## Why weather matters

Traffic paint needs a dry, warm surface to cure properly. Painting in cold or damp conditions can lead to poor adhesion, longer closure times, and lines that wear out faster than they should.

## The sweet spot

Late spring through early fall is generally ideal for most climates — warm, dry days let paint cure quickly, often within an hour or two, minimizing disruption to your lot's operations.

## Planning around your business

We work with property managers to schedule striping during low-traffic windows — early mornings, weekends, or planned closures — so the job gets done with as little disruption as possible.
